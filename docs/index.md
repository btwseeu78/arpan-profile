Senior Platform Engineer with **9 years of experience** architecting and operating enterprise-scale Kubernetes platforms, cloud infrastructure, and GitOps-driven delivery pipelines across GCP, Azure, and on-premises environments.

- **Scale & Reliability:** Specialized in hyper-scale Kubernetes cluster operations (35 GKE clusters, 200+ projects, 10,000+ workloads), cloud-to-cloud and on-prem-to-cloud migrations, and high availability systems (99.9% uptime).
- **Automation & IaC:** Production expertise in Infrastructure as Code (**Terraform**, **Crossplane**), Kubernetes Operators (**Kubebuilder**), and progressive GitOps automation (**ArgoCD**, **Flux**, **Kargo**, **Argo Rollouts**, **Flagger**).
- **Traffic, Ingress & Mesh:** Deep hands-on experience with **Kubernetes Gateway API** (managing 6,000–7,000 URLs), ingress controllers, certificate lifecycle management (**cert-manager**), and disaster recovery (**Velero**).
- **Modern Paradigms:** Active pioneer in **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** for AI-to-infrastructure operations, batch workload queueing (**Kueue**), and multi-cluster management (**Sveltos**).
- **Open Source:** Active contributor to CNCF and Kubernetes SIG projects (`kubernetes-sigs/kueue`, `aquasecurity/trivy-operator`, `GoogleCloudPlatform/kubectl-ai`) and maintainer of `repo-lister`.
